# Changelog

What changed in GTAClipper for people using the product. Newest dates are first.

Add a line here when a change is something a user can see. Use **New**, **Improved**, **Fixed**, or **Under the hood**, describe the change in plain language, and link the pull request when there is one. Do not include customer names, email addresses, user IDs, or anything else private.

## 2026-11-15

- **New:** GTAClipper 1.0. Paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link, or upload a video, and get vertical clips with face-tracked framing, word-synced captions and hook titles. ([Install guide](https://github.com/V413havai/gtaclipper/blob/main/INSTALL.md))
- **New:** Hindi and Hinglish captions. When a caption font can't draw Devanagari, GTAClipper switches to a bundled font that can, so Hindi words never show as empty boxes. ([Captions](https://github.com/V413havai/gtaclipper#whats-inside))
- **New:** The GTAClipper caption style: bold capitals with a yellow highlight on the word being spoken. Works for English, Hindi and Hinglish. ([Captions](https://github.com/V413havai/gtaclipper#whats-inside))
- **New:** Hosted billing can use Razorpay subscriptions (cards and UPI AutoPay) with prices in rupees, alongside Stripe. ([Configuration](https://github.com/V413havai/gtaclipper/blob/main/docs/configuration.md))
- **New:** An optional free monthly allowance (`FREE_PLAN_MINUTE_LIMIT`) for running a free beta or a free tier. ([Configuration](https://github.com/V413havai/gtaclipper/blob/main/docs/configuration.md))
